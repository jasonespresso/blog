Build a Website on Your Phone

1. Connect to the Internet

iOS

  1. Open your Settings app.
  2. Tap Wi-Fi.
  3. Check the Wi-Fi toggle is on.
  4. Tap Connect Community Trust from the list of networks.
  5. Type ConnectCT into the Password field and tap the Enter key on your keyboard. Your device may not actually show the word 'enter' — instead, it might show Go or a symbol of an arrow, or something else entirely.
  6. Return to your Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open your Settings app.
  2. Tap Network and Internet.
  3. Tap Internet.
  4. Check the Wi-Fi toggle is on.
  5. Tap Connect Community Trust from the list of networks.
  6. Type ConnectCT into the Password field and tap the Enter key on your keyboard. Your device may not actually show the word 'enter' — instead, it might show Go or a symbol of an arrow, or something else entirely.
  7. Return to your Home Screen.

2. Open Your Web Browser

You will have at least one of the apps listed below already on your phone — choose the one you recognise:

  • Google Chrome (but not the Google app)
  • Samsung Internet
  • DuckDuckGo
  • Safari
  • Firefox
  • Brave

3. Create a GitHub Account

  1. Type https://github.com into the address bar of your browser and tap Enter.
  2. Tap Sign up for GitHub.
  3. Tap Accept if asked about your cookie preferences.
  4. Tap the Email field and type your email address.
  5. Tap the Password field and create a strong password in accordance with the instructions onscreen.
  6. Tap the Username field and type a username — this will form part of your website address (the name you create must be unique).
  7. Tap Create account — save your password if prompted.

4. Verify Your Account

  1. Open your email app and look for a mail from GitHub
  2. Tap the mail to open it, find the passcode and note it down.
  3. Return to your browser and on the GitHub page, tap the first empty box and type the verification code.
  4. You should now be taken to the GitHub sign in page — enter your username (or email address) and password, then tap Sign in. Alternatively, if your are taken directly to the GitHub homepage, look for the Sign in button.

5. Create Your First Webpage

  1. Tap the + button.
  2. Tap New repository from the menu.
  3. Tap Repository name — think of this as one section of your website. For example, choose one such as recipes or blog or photos, and use all lowercase letters.
  4. Don't worry about all the other settings, just scroll down and tap Create repository.
  5. Scroll to the section that says Quick setup — if you've done this kind of thing before and tap the creating a new file link.
  6. Tap Name your file... and type index.html (all lowercase) as the name of the file.
  7. Scroll down and tap where it says Enter file contents here. Now we begin building your site with HTML — Hypertext Markup Language.
  8. Tap the Commit changes... button, then tap it one more time.
  9. Next tap Settings — if you cannot see it, tap the More menu instead, then tap Settings.
  10. Under the Code and automation heading, tap Pages.
  11. Scroll down and under the Build and deployment heading, look for Source — check that it's set to Deploy from a branch.
  12. Under the Branch heading, tap None and select main. It should now display something like main / (root).
  13. After about 30 seconds, refresh the page and look for the GitHub Pages heading.
  14. Look for the section that begins Your site is live at and tap Visit site.
  15. Congratulations — you've built a website! You can copy the address (known as the URL) and share it with others.
